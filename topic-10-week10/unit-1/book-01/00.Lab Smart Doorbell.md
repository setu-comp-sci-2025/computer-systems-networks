# Lab Smart Doorbell Camera 

In this lab you will build a **“smart doorbell”** app. There are 3 parts to this lab:
**Part 1(all on the RPi):** 

- Press the **Sense HAT joystick** 
- Raspberry Pi **Camera v3** captures a photo 
- The app saves **“last visitor”** info 
- A small **web page** on the Pi shows the last visitor photo + timestamp.

> By the end you’ll have a URL like:  
> `http://hdiprpi:8000/` showing the last person who pressed the button.

**Part 2(Deployed):** 

Extend Part 1 to do the following:

+ The app saves "last visitor" photo to cloud file storage with a public URL
+ Deploy Web Dashboard to Render that shows last visitor photo and timestamp

> By the end you’ll have a URL like:  
> `https://smart-doorbell.onrender.com/` showing the last person who pressed the button.

**Part 3(Event Driven):** 

Extend Part 2 to do the following:

+ Publish "last visitor" info to a MQTT broker
+ Update Web Dashboard app to subscribe to MQTT broker. 

> By the end of part 3 you will have a decoupled Event Driven application. 

---

## Prerequisites

Hardware:

- **Raspberry Pi 4** (any RAM size)
- **Raspberry Pi Camera Module 3** connected to the CSI port
- **Sense HAT** attached to the GPIO header
- Network connectivity (Ethernet or Wi-Fi)

Software:

- **Raspberry Pi OS (Trixie)** (Debian 13–based) installed and booting
- Camera interface enabled
